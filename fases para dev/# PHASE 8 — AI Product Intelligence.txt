# PHASE 8 — AI Product Intelligence, Listing Optimization & Decision Automation

## Project Context

Phases 1 through 7 established the operational and analytical foundation of the commerce platform.

### Phase 1 — Catalog Migration

Existing marketplace listings were extracted and migrated.

### Phase 2 — Central Product Database

Supabase became the central source of truth for product information.

### Phase 3 — Product Management

A private admin panel was created to manage products, images, variants, metadata, and sales-channel selection.

### Phase 4 — Marketplace Publishing

Products can be published to explicitly selected marketplaces.

### Phase 5 — Synchronization & Reconciliation

Marketplace listings can be updated, monitored, reconciled, and verified against canonical Supabase data.

### Phase 6 — Unified Orders & Inventory

Orders from multiple channels can be normalized into Supabase, connected to products, reflected in inventory, and managed through production and fulfillment.

### Phase 7 — Financial Intelligence & Analytics

The system can analyze revenue, costs, fees, margins, product performance, marketplace performance, trends, and profitability.

Phase 8 adds an AI intelligence layer on top of this entire architecture.

The AI layer should not replace the underlying systems.

It should use them.

The core architecture remains:

**Supabase = Single Source of Truth**

AI reads from trusted structured data and produces recommendations, generated content, classifications, rankings, forecasts, and suggested actions.

---

# MAIN OBJECTIVE

Build an AI-powered product and business intelligence layer that helps me:

* Create products faster
* Generate better listing content
* Improve marketplace-specific titles and descriptions
* Generate useful tags and keywords
* Classify products automatically
* Improve search metadata
* Detect weak listings
* Identify promising products
* Identify underperforming products
* Recommend which products to scale
* Recommend which products to improve
* Suggest pricing changes
* Detect opportunities in existing sales data
* Forecast likely demand
* Support catalog expansion
* Reduce repetitive manual work

The AI should behave as an assistant to the business system.

It should not silently make high-impact decisions without control.

---

# CORE PRINCIPLE

AI suggestions and business facts are different things.

The system must always distinguish between:

**Canonical Data**

and:

**AI Suggestions**

Examples:

Canonical price:

`$24.99`

AI recommended price:

`$27.99`

These are NOT the same value.

The AI recommendation should never silently overwrite the canonical price.

Likewise:

Canonical title:

`Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Black Belt Shirt`

AI Suggested Depop Title:

`Funny BJJ Black Belt Graphic Tee`

The suggestion should require approval before becoming canonical or marketplace-specific data.

# STEP 6 — Marketplace-Specific Titles

AI should understand that marketplaces have different listing behaviors and constraints.

For example:

Website title may prioritize clarity and brand consistency.

Depop title may prioritize style and discovery.

Vinted title may prioritize product clarity and searchable attributes.

eBay title may prioritize structured keyword coverage.

The AI system should create marketplace-specific suggestions without modifying the canonical title unless explicitly approved.

# STEP 43 — AI Experiment Suggestions

AI may suggest experiments.

Example:

---

# STEP 44 — Experiment Guardrails

Do not allow the AI to run unlimited uncontrolled experiments.

Set rules such as:

* Maximum active experiments per product
* Minimum duration
* Minimum sample size
* No simultaneous major price/title/image changes unless intentionally designed

Otherwise results become impossible to interpret.

# STEP 78 — Business Rule Layer

# STEP 84 — Privacy and Security

AI services should receive only the data necessary for each task.

Do not unnecessarily send:

* Customer addresses
* Sensitive order information
* Authentication tokens
* Marketplace credentials

Product intelligence generally does not require private customer data.

Minimize data exposure.

---

# STEP 85 — Financial Safety

AI financial recommendations are decision support.

Do not allow AI to silently:

* Change financial records
* Delete expenses
* Modify confirmed marketplace fees
* Change refund history
* Rewrite historical order economics

Financial source records remain protected.

**Approval**

↓

**Publishing/Sync Job**

↓

**Marketplace Connector**

# STEP 87 — AI Failure Handling

If an AI request fails:

Do not block core commerce operations.

The platform must continue functioning without AI.

AI is an enhancement layer, not a critical dependency for:

* Orders
* Inventory
* Publishing
* Fulfillment
* Payments
* Core product management

---

# STEP 88 — Graceful Degradation

If AI is unavailable:

Product creation should still work manually.

Search should still work.

Orders should still work.

Marketplace publishing should still work.

Analytics should still work.

This is a critical architectural requirement.

# STEP 94 — Definition of Done

Phase 8 is complete when:

* AI can assist product creation.
* AI can generate reviewable titles and descriptions.
* AI can suggest tags and search metadata.
* Marketplace-specific content can be generated.
* Suggestions are stored separately from canonical data.
* AI suggestions are auditable.
* AI can analyze product performance using real business data.
* AI can identify opportunities and risks.
* AI can suggest experiments.
* AI can support demand forecasting where sufficient data exists.
* AI can help identify inventory/production risks.
* Important actions require approval.
* AI cannot bypass publishing/synchronization controls.
* AI cannot silently overwrite financial truth.
* AI can fail without breaking core operations.
* Recommendation outcomes can be measured.
