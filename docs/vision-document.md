# Project Vision Document

## Project

Premium Customization Platform for Jiu-Jitsu Academies

## Vision

Create the leading apparel and product customization platform for the jiu-jitsu market, offering a premium digital experience that combines speed, quality, simplicity, and customization.

The platform won't just be a product catalog, but a tool connecting academies, event organizers, and athletes to a modern process of creating, approving, and producing custom products.

Our goal is to transform an operational need — producing custom t-shirts and products — into a pleasant, professional, and efficient experience.

## Mission

Enable any academy or event organizer to develop high-quality custom products without bureaucracy, with no minimum order, and with one of the fastest production times in the market.

## Long-Term Vision

To be recognized as the benchmark platform for sports customization, starting with the jiu-jitsu market and later expanding to other segments such as companies, schools, gyms, corporate events, and sports teams.

In the future, each client could have their own storefront within the platform, managing their products while utilizing our production and logistics infrastructure.

## Problem

Today, most academies face difficulties producing custom products due to factors such as:

- High minimum orders;
- Long lead times;
- Communication difficulties;
- Slow quoting processes;
- Lack of transparency during production;
- Low presentation quality from suppliers.

Furthermore, many suppliers have limited digital presence, conveying little trust and making client decision-making harder.

## Solution

Develop a modern digital platform where academies can:

- Get to know the company;
- View professional models;
- Find inspiration;
- Quickly request a quote;
- Send their visual identity;
- Start custom projects in minutes.

The entire process should be intuitive, fast, and designed to reduce friction between the first contact and closing the order.

## Value Proposition

Our brand will be built on four pillars:

### Fast Production

Agile deliveries, efficient communication, and optimized processes to reduce the time between artwork approval and product delivery.

### No Minimum Order

The client can produce from a single piece up to large volumes, without minimum requirements.

### Total Customization

Each academy can create exclusive products using their visual identity, colors, sponsors, and custom graphic elements.

We won't just work with printing; we will help our clients build a brand identity.

### Premium Quality

All products must convey a high standard of finish.

The platform will initially work with Gildan shirts, Premium DTF printing, and carefully selected materials to offer excellent durability, comfort, and presentation.

### Personas

#### 🥋 Professor Rafael — Beginner Academy

- **Context:** Just opened his academy, 15-30 students. Building everything from scratch — visual identity, digital presence, community.
- **Need:** Shirts with the academy logo to create a sense of belonging among the first students.
- **Main Pain Point:** Can't order in large quantities because he still has few students. Most suppliers require a minimum order of 30-50 pieces.
- **Buying Behavior:** Small orders (5-15 pieces), low frequency (1-2x/year), limited budget. Highly values "no minimum order".
- **What wins him over:** Simple process, small orders accepted, professional result that elevates the academy's perception.

#### 🏛️ Professor Marcos — Consolidated Academy

- **Context:** Established academy for 5+ years, 80-200 students. Already has a defined visual identity, logo, brand colors.
- **Need:** Produce shirts for belt promotions, internal events, student merchandise, welcome kits.
- **Main Pain Point:** Current supplier takes too long, communication is poor, and quality varies between orders.
- **Buying Behavior:** Medium to large orders (30-100 pieces), regular frequency (3-4x/year), comfortable budget. Values consistent quality and fast turnaround.
- **What wins him over:** Quality consistency, production speed, professional service, and a platform that conveys trust.

#### 🎨 Professor Ana — Focus on Branding

- **Context:** Academy with strong visual identity, active Instagram presence, focus on student experience.
- **Need:** Shirts that act as a brand extension — it's not just a shirt, it's a lifestyle product students want to wear outside the gym.
- **Main Pain Point:** Generic suppliers don't understand branding. Deliver shirts with poorly positioned prints, mismatched colors, and don't help with creation.
- **Buying Behavior:** Varied orders, high demand for design and finish. Willing to pay more for premium quality. Values total customization and visual consulting.
- **What wins her over:** Professional design, attention to detail, ability to create products that look like an established brand.

#### 🏆 Professor Diego — Competition Team

- **Context:** Leads a competition team with 10-30 athletes. Travels to regional and national tournaments.
- **Need:** Team shirts for tournaments — uniforms that identify the team, with sponsor names and competitive visual identity.
- **Main Pain Point:** Tight deadline between event confirmation and the tournament. Needs fast production without complications.
- **Buying Behavior:** Medium orders (10-30 pieces), frequency per event (4-8x/year), budget varies by tournament. Values speed above all else.
- **What wins him over:** Fast production, impactful visual result, bureaucracy-free process.

## Commercial Strategy

The platform will be a sales tool, operating across multiple channels.

### In-Person Channel

During in-person visits to academies, the representative will hand out a flyer along with a 3D-printed wristband containing an NFC chip programmed with the platform link.

By tapping their phone to the wristband, the client instantly accesses the platform.

> **Note:** NFC is just a physical commercial presentation accessory. There is no technical integration with the platform — the chip simply contains the website URL.

### SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

SEO will be a strong pillar for organic acquisition. The platform must be optimized to rank in search engines for niche terms.

- **Target keywords:** "custom jiu-jitsu shirts", "bjj academy apparel", "custom team shirts no minimum", "dtf printed martial arts clothing"
- **Technical SEO:** SSR via Next.js, dynamic meta tags per page, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, schema markup (Product, Organization)
- **Content SEO:** Unique titles and descriptions per product, friendly URLs, alt text on all images
- **Core Web Vitals:** Goals defined in the mobile performance section (LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1)
- **Optimized pages:** Each category and product should be a rankable landing page

### Social Proof (Instagram / Social Media)

- Display real photos of finished products and testimonials from served academies on the platform
- Integration with Instagram feed (display recent posts on the Home or gallery section)
- Encourage clients to post photos with the brand hashtag

### Referral Program + Discount Coupons

- **Discount coupons:** The platform must generate and validate discount coupons with configurable rules (percentage, fixed amount, validity, single or multiple use, minimum quantity)
- **Referral:** Each academy served receives an exclusive coupon to refer other academies
- **First-time purchase coupons:** Offer discounts for new clients arriving via SEO or social media
- **Admin management:** The admin panel should allow creating, editing, deactivating, and monitoring coupon usage

### Google Ads (Future)

- Campaigns for high-intent terms: "custom bjj shirts near me", "personalized gym apparel"
- Retargeting for visitors who did not complete the quote form

### Pricing Strategy

| Quantity Range | Price per Piece | Gross Margin |
|----------------|-----------------|--------------|
| 1 – 10 pieces | $20.00 | 75.0% |
| 11 – 50 pieces | $18.00 | 72.2% |
| 51 – 100 pieces | $15.00 | 66.7% |
| 100+ pieces | On request | Min. 60% |

### Feasibility of "No Minimum Order"

The minimum gross margin of **66.7%** (even in the most aggressive scenario of $15/piece) sustains the no-minimum-order model. In-house production (own DTF equipment) eliminates reliance on third parties and allows full volume flexibility.

The tiered pricing by quantity range incentivizes larger orders without making single orders unfeasible.

### Target Gross Margin

- **Goal:** 60% – 70%
- **Minimum acceptable:** 60%
- **Monthly fixed costs** (hosting, domain, tools): Absorbed by overall operations, not passed on per piece.
